YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how StudySpark, a student flashcard study application developed as a CPP123 Emerging Technology course project, handles information. It applies to this starter version. The developer must review it before publication and update it if the app's behavior or hosting setup changes.

1. Information stored by StudySpark

When you use StudySpark, you may enter flashcard questions, answers, subject deck names, and whether a card is marked as remembered. This information is saved in your browser's local storage on the device and browser you use. This version does not require an account and does not request your name, email address, or password.

2. How information is used

The app uses saved flashcards to display subject decks, study cards, search results, and progress counts. In this version, the app code does not send your flashcard content to a StudySpark server or database.

3. Storage and deletion

Your flashcards remain in local storage in the current browser profile. You can delete individual flashcards or subject decks inside the app. Clearing this website's browser storage may delete all saved decks and cards. Local data does not automatically move to another browser or device. The developer does not have a server-side copy to retrieve or delete in this version.

4. Third-party services

The stylesheet loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser may contact Google's font service to download font files. That service may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address. If this site is published using a hosting provider, that provider may process technical request data or access logs under its own privacy policy. Review the actual hosting provider's terms before publication.

5. Security and limitations

Browser storage is provided for convenience and is not designed for sensitive or confidential information. Someone with access to your browser profile may be able to see your saved cards. Avoid entering passwords, student identification numbers, confidential course materials, or other sensitive personal data. Data can be lost if browser storage is cleared or the device is changed. No storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children's and other people's information

Do not enter sensitive personal information about yourself or other people. Use fictional or non-sensitive examples when demonstrating this course project.

7. Policy changes

This policy must be updated if the app adds user accounts, analytics, a cloud database, or other services that change its data practices. The date above should reflect the latest revision.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the student developer through the contact method approved by the course instructor. Before publishing, replace this sentence with a suitable contact method or revise it to accurately explain how privacy questions can be raised.

Student developer note: Confirm this policy matches the final deployed website and the hosting provider's actual practices. Include the complete published policy in the project report.
