YOUR INFORMATION MATTERS

Privacy Policy

✦ Last updated: October 24, 2026

StudySpark is a browser-based flashcard study application created as a CPP123 Emerging Technology course project. This policy explains what information the app stores and the role of the services used to publish it.

01Information stored in the app

You may enter flashcard questions, answers, subject or deck names, and study progress. StudySpark stores this information in your browser's local storage so your cards can remain available when you refresh the page. The app does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, or password.

02How information is used

The flashcard information you enter is used to display cards and decks, support searching and studying, and show study progress. The StudySpark app code stores this content in your browser and does not send your flashcard content to a StudySpark-owned server or database.

03Storage and deletion

Your saved cards are stored in local storage in your browser profile and on your device. You can remove cards or decks using the app's available controls. Clearing this website's local storage or browser data may remove saved cards. Your data does not automatically transfer to another browser or device. The developer cannot recover cards from a StudySpark server because the app does not keep a server-side copy.

04Hosting and third-party services

The website is published using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with requests to its hosting service, such as IP addresses, browser details, and access logs, according to its own policies. The website also loads its stylesheet from the same website and may request font files from Google Fonts if configured to do so. Third-party providers control their own data practices; please review their current privacy policies for details.

05Security and limitations

Local browser storage is intended for convenience and is not a secure vault for confidential information. People with access to your browser profile or device may be able to access saved cards. Information may be lost if browser data is cleared, the browser profile is removed, or you change devices. No website or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

06Information about other people

Do not enter passwords, student identification numbers, sensitive personal information, or confidential information about yourself or other people. Use fictional or non-sensitive sample content when demonstrating this course project.

07Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if StudySpark's features, storage methods, hosting, or third-party services change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

08Contact

For privacy questions about this student course project, please contact the student developer through the course instructor.
